# Blueprint: Online Giving & Stewardship

## Page Overview
* **Purpose**: Facilitate secure digital tithing, offerings, and missions giving with vision-driven messaging ("Partner With What God Is Doing").
* **Target Users**: Active church members and supporters.
* **Main Goal**: Provide secure, multi-fund digital giving options.

## Structure
1. Frequency selector (One-Time vs Recurring Monthly).
2. Fund designation (General Tithes, Food Pantry Outreach, Global Missions, Building Fund).
3. Preset & custom amount selector.
4. 256-bit SSL security badge and FAQs.
