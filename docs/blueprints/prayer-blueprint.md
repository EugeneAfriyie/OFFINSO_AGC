# Blueprint: Prayer Requests & Pastoral Care

## Page Overview
* **Purpose**: Offer a safe, encouraging portal for visitors to submit public or confidential prayer requests.
* **Target Users**: Anyone experiencing illness, trial, or seeking pastoral prayer.
* **Main Goal**: Provide accessible pastoral care and intercession.

## Structure
1. Encouraging promise header ("You Are Not Alone").
2. Confidentiality toggle (Pastors Only vs Prayer Team).
3. Instant submission confirmation feedback.
