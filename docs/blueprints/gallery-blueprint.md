# Blueprint: Photo & Video Gallery

## Page Overview
* **Purpose**: Showcase authentic photography of worship services, baptisms, youth camps, and outreach.
* **Target Users**: Seekers and families visualizing church atmosphere.
* **Main Goal**: Communicate visual warmth, joy, and authenticity.

## Structure
1. Filterable photo grid (Worship, Baptisms, Youth, Outreach, Fellowship).
2. Lightbox image preview popup.
